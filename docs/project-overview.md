# Project Overview

## Church Of Christ — கிறிஸ்துவின் சபை

A bilingual (Tamil / English) church website for the **Church Of Christ** in
Dindukkal, Tamil Nadu, India.

**வேதமே வெளிச்சம் — The Word is Light.**

The website introduces the church to its congregation and visitors, sharing
service times, ministries, leadership, events, and a way to plan a visit. It
is designed to be warm, reverent, and accessible, with a full Tamil ↔ English
language switch.

## Purpose

- Give the congregation a single trusted place for service times and church
  information.
- Introduce new visitors to the church's ministries, leadership, and values.
- Prepare the ground for future features such as songs, videos, prayer
  requests, and contact forms.

## Current status

The **frontend** is implemented (React + Vite + Tailwind CSS). The
**backend and database are planned but not yet implemented** — see
[`architecture.md`](architecture.md) and [`requirements.md`](requirements.md).
