# Requirements

## Current (implemented in the frontend)

- Bilingual content — Tamil and English, with a working language switcher
  (preference persisted in `localStorage`).
- Homepage sections:
  - Hero
  - About the church
  - Service times
  - Ministries
  - Latest video
  - Leadership
  - Daily Bible verse
  - Prayer call-to-action
  - Visit us (address + map directions)
- Responsive, accessible design with reduced-motion support.
- Sticky header with mobile menu.
- All imagery served from the frontend bundle.

## Planned (not yet implemented)

- **Songs / Lyrics** page and management.
- **Videos** library and metadata.
- **Daily Bible verse** configuration (if needed).
- **Prayer requests** submission.
- **Contact messages** form and inbox.
- **Church content management** (news, events, announcements).
- **Admin authentication**.
- **Media management** (uploads).

## Non-goals (for now)

- No backend, database, authentication, or third-party services are
  implemented yet. These are design placeholders only.
